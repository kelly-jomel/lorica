Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Angel of God, my guardian dear, to whom God's love commits me here:
Ever this night be at my side, to light and guard, to rule and guide.
Defend my sleeping mind from all terrifying dreams, nocturnal phantoms, and troubling thoughts.
Stand watch at the head of my bed until the dawn breaks, and offer my quiet breathing as incense before God's throne. Amen.
