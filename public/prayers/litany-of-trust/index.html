Complete Traditional Prayer Text

From the belief that I have to earn Your love... Jesus, deliver me.
From the fear that I am unlovable... Jesus, deliver me.
From the false security that I have what it takes... Jesus, deliver me.
From the fear that trusting You will leave me more destitute... Jesus, deliver me.
From all suspicion of Your words and promises... Jesus, deliver me.
That You are continually holding me, sustaining me, loving me... Jesus, I trust in You.
That Your love goes deeper than my sins and falls... Jesus, I trust in You.
That You will not leave me an orphan... Jesus, I trust in You.
