About Lorica

A traditional Catholic spiritual armor sanctuary engineered for peace, contemplation, and spiritual perseverance.

Our Sacred Mission

Lorica (from the Latin word for breastplate or protective cuirass) was created to offer every soul free, immediate access to authentic Catholic prayer without distractions, algorithmic paywalls, or third-party ads. Rooted in the rich patrimony of Holy Mother Church, Lorica unites historical Latin devotions with reverent studio voice narrations and Gregorian choral accompaniment.

What Lorica Provides

  • 46 Traditional Catholic Prayers: Spanning Morning Offerings, Night Compline, Marian devotions, and Liturgical Latin.
  • 12 Complete 9-Day Novenas: Paired with daily 5-decade Holy Rosary meditations.
  • Spiritual Warfare Armor: The St. Michael deliverance prayer, St. Benedict exorcism, and loopable Psalm 91 audio for night battles.
  • Catholic Examination of Conscience: Step-by-step guidance for the Sacrament of Penance and Reconciliation.
  • Machine & Agent Ready: Native support for WebMCP, RFC 8288 link headers, and structured JSON-LD schemas.
