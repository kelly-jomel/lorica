St. Andrew Christmas Anticipation Novena (Complete 9-Day Novena)

Pray the complete 9 days of the St. Andrew Christmas Anticipation Novena with full liturgical prayers, scriptures, 5-decade Holy Rosary, and studio audio recordings.

Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Day 1: The Mystery of the Nativity

Intention: For a special Christmas intention and spiritual renewal.

Luke 2:7 — 'And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold. In that hour vouchsafe, O my God, to hear my prayer and grant my desires, through the merits of Our Savior Jesus Christ, and of His blessed Mother. Amen. (Prayed 15 times daily throughout Advent).

Day 2: Preparation for the King

Intention: Cleanse the manger of our hearts through Confession.

Isaiah 40:3 — 'Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight in the wilderness the paths of our God.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold. Hear my prayer, O Lord.

Day 3: The Humility of the Stable

Intention: Deliverance from greed and worldly pride.

Philippians 2:7 — 'He emptied himself, taking the form of a servant.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold. In that hour grant my prayer.

Day 4: The Shepherds' Vigil

Intention: For the poor, homeless, and forgotten.

Luke 2:8 — 'And there were in the same country shepherds watching, and keeping the night watches over their flock.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold.

Day 5: The Heavenly Host Song

Intention: For peace in war-torn lands and reconciliation.

Luke 2:14 — 'Glory to God in the highest; and on earth peace to men of good will.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold.

Day 6: The Light in the Darkness

Intention: For family members who have lost their faith.

John 1:5 — 'And the light shineth in darkness, and the darkness did not comprehend it.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold.

Day 7: The Star of Bethlehem

Intention: For clarity of vocation and life purpose.

Matthew 2:2 — 'For we have seen his star in the east, and are come to adore him.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold.

Day 8: The Adoration of the Magi

Intention: Offering our gold, frankincense, and myrrh to Jesus.

Matthew 2:11 — 'And falling down they adored him.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold.

Day 9: The Dawning of the Savior

Intention: Welcoming Christ into our home and eternity.

John 1:14 — 'And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us.'

Hail and blessed be the hour and moment in which the Son of God was born of the most pure Virgin Mary, at midnight, in Bethlehem, in piercing cold. In that hour vouchsafe, O my God, to hear my prayer and grant my desires. Amen.
