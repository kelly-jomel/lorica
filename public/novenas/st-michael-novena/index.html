St. Michael the Archangel 9-Day Novena (Complete 9-Day Novena)

Pray the complete 9 days of the St. Michael the Archangel 9-Day Novena with full liturgical prayers, scriptures, 5-decade Holy Rosary, and studio audio recordings.

Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Day 1: Day 1: Champion of God's Honor

Intention: For personal purity and the grace of humility before God.

Revelation 12:7 — 'There was a great battle in heaven: Michael and his angels fought with the dragon.'

St. Michael the Archangel, we honor you as a powerful protector of the Church and guardian of our souls. Inspire us with your humility, courage and strength that we may reject sin and perfect our love for our Heavenly Father. In your strength and humility, slay the evil and pride in our hearts so that nothing will keep us from God. St. Michael the Archangel, pray that we may be blessed by God with the zeal to live our lives in accordance with Christ’s teachings. St. Michael the Archangel, you are the prince of angels but in your humility you recognized that God is God and you are but His servant. Unlike satan, you were not overcome with pride but were steadfast in humility. Pray that we will have this same humility. It is in the spirit of that humility that we ask for your intercession for our petitions… (state your petitions)

Day 2: Day 2: Guardian of Families & Children

Intention: For the protection of our homes and peace among spouses and children.

Psalm 91:11 — 'For he hath given his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 3: Day 3: Strength in Acute Temptation

Intention: For deliverance from habitual sins and intrusive mental oppression.

1 Peter 5:8 — 'Be sober and watch: because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 4: Day 4: Consoler of the Sick & Dying

Intention: For sick relatives, those in hospital, and the agonizing.

Daniel 12:1 — 'At that time shall Michael rise up, the great prince, who standeth for the children of thy people.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 5: Day 5: Guardian of the Church & Priesthood

Intention: For our parish priests, bishops, and the universal Church.

Ephesians 6:12 — 'For our wrestling is not against flesh and blood, but against principalities and powers.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 6: Day 6: Deliverance from Fear & Nighttime Dread

Intention: For deep, restful sleep and freedom from anxious panic.

Psalm 27:3 — 'If armies in camp should stand together against me, my heart shall not fear.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 7: Day 7: Shield Against Malice & Slander

Intention: For forgiveness of enemies and protection against false accusations.

Romans 8:31 — 'If God be for us, who is against us?'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 8: Day 8: Patron of the Dying & Spiritual Victory

Intention: For final perseverance and victory at the hour of death.

2 Timothy 4:7 — 'I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen

Day 9: Day 9: Thanksgiving & Perpetual Consecration

Intention: Consecration of oneself, home, and loved ones to St. Michael.

Jude 1:9 — 'When Michael the archangel, disputing with the devil, said: The Lord command thee.'

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.
