Novena for the Holy Souls in Purgatory (Complete 9-Day Novena)

Pray the complete 9 days of the Novena for the Holy Souls in Purgatory with full liturgical prayers, scriptures, 5-decade Holy Rosary, and studio audio recordings.

Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Day 1: Day 1: For the Most Abandoned Souls

Intention: For souls who have no one on earth to pray for them.

Psalm 130:1 — 'Out of the depths I have cried to thee, O Lord.'

O Lord God Almighty, I beseech Thee by the Precious Blood which Thy Divine Son shed in Gethsemane, deliver the souls in purgatory, especially the most forgotten. Requiem aeternam dona eis, Domine.

Day 2: Day 2: For Departed Parents & Ancestors

Intention: In thanksgiving and reparation for our parents.

Exodus 20:12 — 'Honour thy father and thy mother.'

Jesus, Savior of the world, look with mercy upon the souls of our deceased parents, grandparents, and ancestors. Forgive them their human frailties.

Day 3: Day 3: For Deceased Spouses & Children

Intention: Consolation for grieving hearts.

Wisdom 3:1 — 'The souls of the just are in the hand of God, and the torment of death shall not touch them.'

Lord Jesus, You wept at the tomb of Lazarus. Comfort all grieving spouses and parents, and bring our departed loved ones into eternal light.

Day 4: Day 4: For Deceased Priests & Religious

Intention: For bishops and pastors who administered the sacraments.

Hebrews 5:1 — 'Every high priest taken from among men, is ordained for men in the things that appertain to God.'

Eternal High Priest, admit into the heavenly liturgy all deceased priests who baptized, absolved, and fed us with Your Body and Blood.

Day 5: Day 5: For Those Who Died Suddenly or Unprepared

Intention: For victims of accidents, violence, and despair.

Matthew 24:44 — 'Be you also ready: because at what hour you know not the Son of man will come.'

Merciful Lord, show boundless mercy to souls taken without the Last Rites. Wash them clean in the fountain of Calvary.

Day 6: Day 6: For Souls Closest to Release

Intention: That their hours of waiting may be ended.

Psalm 42:2 — 'My soul hath thirsted after the strong living God: when shall I come and appear before the face of God?'

Lord Jesus, shorten the fiery exile of the souls nearest to heaven. Open the gates of paradise to them today.

Day 7: Day 7: For Those Suffering for Worldly Attachments

Intention: Purification from vanity and love of riches.

1 Corinthians 3:15 — 'He himself shall be saved, yet so as by fire.'

Lamb of God, cleanse these souls from all earthly stains. Bestow upon them the white garment of the redeemed.

Day 8: Day 8: For the Holy Souls in Reparation for Sins of the Tongue

Intention: For slander, gossip, and harsh judgments.

Matthew 12:36 — 'Every idle word that men shall speak, they shall render an account for it in the day of judgment.'

Divine Redeemer, purify those who suffer for uncharitable words. May they now sing Your praises forever with the angels.

Day 9: Day 9: The Vision of God (Lux Aeterna)

Intention: Eternal rest and perpetual light.

Revelation 21:4 — 'And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes: and death shall be no more.'

Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May they rest in peace. Amen.
