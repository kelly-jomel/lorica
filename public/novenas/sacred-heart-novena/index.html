Novena to the Sacred Heart of Jesus (Complete 9-Day Novena)

Pray the complete 9 days of the Novena to the Sacred Heart of Jesus with full liturgical prayers, scriptures, 5-decade Holy Rosary, and studio audio recordings.

Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Day 1: Day 1: Ask and You Shall Receive

Intention: For deep confidence in the promises of Christ.

Matthew 7:7 — 'Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and you shall find: knock, and it shall be opened to you.'

O my Jesus, You have said: 'Truly I say to you, ask and you shall receive, seek and you shall find, knock and it shall be opened to you.' Behold I knock, I seek and I ask for the grace of... Sacred Heart of Jesus, I place all my trust in You.

Day 2: Day 2: Whatsoever You Ask in My Name

Intention: Petitioning the Father through the Sacred Heart.

John 14:13 — 'Whatever you shall ask the Father in my name, that will I do.'

O my Jesus, You have said: 'Truly I say to you, if you ask anything of the Father in My name, He will give it to you.' Behold, in Your name, I ask the Father for the grace of... Sacred Heart of Jesus, I place all my trust in You.

Day 3: Day 3: Heaven and Earth Shall Pass Away

Intention: Relying on the eternal fidelity of God's Word.

Matthew 24:35 — 'Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass.'

O my Jesus, You have said: 'Truly I say to you, heaven and earth will pass away but My words will not pass away.' Encouraged by Your infallible words I now ask for the grace of... Sacred Heart of Jesus, I place all my trust in You.

Day 4: Day 4: Fount of All Consolation

Intention: For relief in emotional grief and trials.

Matthew 11:28 — 'Come to me, all you that labour, and are burdened, and I will refresh you.'

O Sacred Heart of Jesus, to Whom it is impossible not to have compassion on the afflicted, have pity on us miserable sinners and grant us the grace which we ask of You. St. Joseph, foster father of Jesus, pray for us.

Day 5: Day 5: Sanctuary of Divine Love

Intention: Consecration of families to Christ's Heart.

John 19:34 — 'One of the soldiers with a spear opened his side, and immediately there came out blood and water.'

O Heart of Love, I put all my trust in You; for I fear all things from my own weakness, but I hope for all things from Your goodness. Sacred Heart of Jesus, I believe in Your love for me.

Day 6: Day 6: Refuge in Temptation

Intention: For victory over spiritual darkness.

1 Corinthians 10:13 — 'God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that which you are able.'

Heart of Jesus, burning furnace of charity, consume in me all that is displeasing to You. Defend me against all demonic snares and lead me into eternal life.

Day 7: Day 7: Peace in the Home

Intention: For marital unity and reconciliation.

Colossians 3:15 — 'And let the peace of Christ rejoice in your hearts.'

Sacred Heart of Jesus, King and center of all hearts, establish Your peace in our family. Bless our home and dwell with us forever.

Day 8: Day 8: Comfort for the Agonizing

Intention: For the dying and departed.

Luke 23:43 — 'Amen I say to thee, this day thou shalt be with me in paradise.'

Heart of Jesus, in Whom are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge, be the salvation of those who are in their final agony this day.

Day 9: Day 9: Total Consecration

Intention: Offering of life, works, and death to the Sacred Heart.

Romans 8:39 — 'Nor any other creature shall be able to separate us from the love of God.'

O Sacred Heart of Jesus, I consecrate to Thee my being and my life, my actions, pains, and sufferings. Be Thou my love, my shield, and the repairer of all my defects. Sacred Heart of Jesus, I place all my trust in Thee. Amen.
