The Miraculous Medal Perpetual Novena (Complete 9-Day Novena)

Pray the complete 9 days of the The Miraculous Medal Perpetual Novena with full liturgical prayers, scriptures, 5-decade Holy Rosary, and studio audio recordings.

Complete Traditional Prayer Text

Day 1: Day 1: O Mary Conceived Without Sin

Intention: For personal purity and victory over temptation.

Genesis 3:15 — 'I will put enmities between thee and the woman, and thy seed and her seed.'

O Immaculate Virgin Mary, Mother of Our Lord Jesus and our Mother, penetrated with the most lively confidence in your all-powerful and never-failing intercession, we beg you to obtain for us the graces we ask in this novena. O Mary, conceived without sin, pray for us who have recourse to thee.

Day 2: Day 2: Rays of Grace from Mary's Hands

Intention: For all graces that souls neglect to ask for.

Proverbs 8:35 — 'He that shall find me, shall find life, and shall have salvation from the Lord.'

O Mary, you showed St. Catherine rays of light streaming from your hands, symbolizing the graces you shed upon those who ask for them. Cast those rays upon our darkened lives. O Mary, conceived without sin, pray for us who have recourse to thee.

Day 3: Day 3: The Cross and the 'M'

Intention: For love of the Holy Cross and union with Christ's Passion.

Galatians 6:14 — 'God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.'

Virgin Mother, on the reverse of the medal the letter 'M' is entwined with the Cross of Christ. Teach us to embrace our daily crosses in union with your maternal heart.

Day 4: Day 4: The Two Sacred Hearts

Intention: For total consecration to the Sacred Heart of Jesus and Immaculate Heart of Mary.

Luke 2:35 — 'And thy own soul a sword shall pierce.'

O Jesus, whose Heart is encircled with thorns; O Mary, whose heart is pierced by a sword: unite our hearts forever to Yours. Protect our families from all evil.

Day 5: Day 5: Health of Mind and Body

Intention: For sick relatives and those facing medical tests.

Matthew 8:17 — 'He took our infirmities, and bore our diseases.'

O Mary, Health of the Sick, by the miraculous graces poured through your medal, touch our diseased bodies and anxious minds with divine peace.

Day 6: Day 6: Conversion of Sinners

Intention: For family members who have abandoned the sacraments.

Luke 15:7 — 'There shall be joy in heaven upon one sinner that doth penance.'

Refuge of Sinners, obtain the grace of true repentance for our loved ones who have fallen away from the Church. Pierce their hardened hearts with grace.

Day 7: Day 7: Shield Against Enemy Snares

Intention: Wearing the medal as armor against dark forces.

Ephesians 6:11 — 'Put on the armour of God.'

Queen of Heaven, who crushed the head of the ancient serpent, preserve us from all demonic attacks, curses, and witchcraft. Keep our threshold sacred.

Day 8: Day 8: For the Faithful Departed

Intention: For holy souls in purgatory.

Revelation 14:13 — 'Blessed are the dead, who die in the Lord.'

Mother of Mercy, open the gates of heaven for our deceased relatives and friends. Let perpetual light shine upon them.

Day 9: Day 9: Perpetual Consecration

Intention: Lifelong consecration under Our Lady's mantle.

John 19:27 — 'Behold thy mother! And from that hour, the disciple took her to his own.'

O Virgin Mother of God, Mary Immaculate, we dedicate and consecrate ourselves to you under the title of Our Lady of the Miraculous Medal. May this medal be for each one of us a sure sign of your affection and a constant reminder of our duties toward you. Amen.
