Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by Xydris.in

1. Commitment to User Privacy & Sacred Contemplation

Lorica is built to provide an undisturbed, sacred prayer experience. We believe that spiritual prayer and meditation should be completely free of tracking, third-party behavioral profiling, and commercial advertising. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We only process information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Google Sign-In: When you choose to sign in via Google Identity Services to access your Benefactor Portal, we receive your basic profile data (name, email address, and profile photo) as authorized by your Google account. This is used solely to identify your giving ledger and Mass covenant intentions.
  • Donations & Financial Information: All financial transactions are processed securely through PayPal. Lorica does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank credentials, or financial account details on our servers.
  • Local Storage: Your prayer preferences, playback history, volume settings, and novena progress are saved locally on your device via browser LocalStorage.

3. How We Use Information

  • To maintain and display your personal contribution records and active monthly giving agreements.
  • To enroll benefactors in the First Friday Perpetual Mass Intentions Registry.
  • To send direct transaction receipts and automated administrative security notices.

4. Third-Party Services

We work exclusively with trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Google Identity Services: For secure, one-tap authentication.
  • PayPal: For processing voluntary patron offerings and monthly subscriptions.
  • Google Tag Manager / Analytics: For aggregate, non-personally identifiable site traffic metrics.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request the deletion of your account history or removal from the Benefactor registry at any time by contacting our administrator. You may also cancel any recurring PayPal agreement directly from your user portal or PayPal account.
